# What the overlays mean

The HD replay writes the logged full qpos/qvel into independent presentation data and calls `mj_forward`, without integrating new physics or executing a new policy. Each normal frame shows qpos_before at the input tick; an actual final qpos_after state is appended and held 45 video frames with simulation time frozen. That presentation pause adds no grading evidence. PASS/FAIL appears only at the completed terminal state and comes from the original frozen grader.

The cyan TCP trail covers the preceding roughly four simulated seconds. Amber dots are independent forward kinematics of the ACT action chunk after physical actuator clipping. Consumed targets disappear. Solid targets 1–8 are queued for execution, while dashed targets 9–16 are unexecuted predictions that are superseded by the next replan. Labels 1, 4, 8, 12, 16 keep clustered points readable. The chunk spans 16 ticks at 30 Hz; a fresh chunk replaces it every 8 ticks. The projection does not validate collisions, tracking, object motion or a future dynamic trajectory.

Camera projection uses the active overview scene's `mjv_averageCamera` and actual frustum, following [MuJoCo 3.6.0's mono rendering implementation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google-deepmind/mujoco/3.6.0/src/render/classic/render_gl3.c). An independent check renders three isolated world spheres and compares their pixel centroids with the projected coordinates. The largest discrepancy in these clips was 0.186 pixels, below the 1.5 pixel check tolerance. Shadows are disabled for the HD presentation only. Actual logged model-input RGB is preserved in the two 96×96 insets and remains unannotated.

Green tray outlines and live cube height are simulator ground-truth diagnostics. They are not visual detections and never enter the learned policy. Displayed inference milliseconds come from the actual logged experiment, rather than the replay. Gripper opening is expressed as the measured gripper joint's normalized angular range, rather than a calibrated jaw gap in millimetres.

## Cached MuJoCo transforms

The original collector reads `tcp_before_m` and `tcp_after_m` directly from `data.xpos/xmat` after `mj_step`. MuJoCo's Euler step performs forward dynamics and then integrates joint positions; those cached transforms therefore represent the state before the last 1/600 second substep. The overlay refreshes transforms from the authoritative saved qpos. This accounts for a maximum 1.602 mm discrepancy between cached telemetry and refreshed TCP across all 13 episodes. It is a one-physics-substep difference, not a 30 Hz control-tick shift. See the [MuJoCo step and integration source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google-deepmind/mujoco/3.6.0/src/engine/engine_forward.c).

Independent validation checked every saved pre/post state in all 13 episodes: applying `mj_integratePos(qpos,qvel,-timestep)` and refreshing FK reproduced the cached TCP values within 1.67×10⁻¹⁶ m. All 2630 overlay points across five clips exactly matched fresh FK of the saved qpos, including their terminal states. The actual saved RGB inputs and frozen grading trace retain their original cache timing; they were not rewritten. These checks are retained in `runs/lerobot-cases/overlays/tcp-consistency-validation.json` in the release bundle.

The archive also contains the temporal overlay record, exact source/checkpoint/trajectory/video hashes, snapshot hashes, state/action clipping checks and video validation. Each HD clip is 1440×1080, 30 fps, 570 frames / 19 seconds, including the 45 frame completed-state pause. The earlier raw videos remain available for all rollouts, including cases without an annotated counterpart.
