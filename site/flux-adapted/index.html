Trained FLUX: 2/3 fresh simulated starts

The original pretrained model did not complete our two initial simulated placements. We then trained the genuine FLUX 3 Action SO101 model for our scene using 17 demonstrations, reserving three other demonstrations for validation. The selected or latest tested model used 256 optimizer updates. Checkpoint selection and guidance comparisons have separate recorded outcomes.

FLUX learns to turn two camera views, measured joints, previous issued commands and a short instruction into the next joint targets. Training uses the original native flow-matching loss, rank-8 LoRA and trainable action heads. In closed-loop tests it receives no object coordinates or scripted pickup corrections.

Development conditions and fresh starts have separate denominators. The frozen selected model completed 2/3 fresh same-task placements; every planned start is retained. These are small simulation tests; physical SO101, Orin deployment and real-time control remain untested. A 30 fps replay pauses physics while model inference runs.
